# Chiplytics PSA System

## Comprehensive Electrical Inspection for Critical Systems

Power Spectrum Analysis (PSA) is a patented electrical testing method for capturing pseudosignatures of electronic components used to track and verify authenticity throughout the supply chain. Use the Chiplytics PSA System to capture repeatable, calibrated signatures.

## Key Features

#### Quantifiable Assurance

Power Spectrum Analysis (PSA) performs a non-invasive modal analysis of an electrical part, revealing subtle, quantifiable differences such as in performance, usage, or even manufacturers.

#### Repeatable Platform

The Chiplytics PSA System integrates test and measurement capabilities into a compact, repeatable form factor with minimal signal interference. Data processing and device calibration keep signatures consistent over time.

#### Simple Interface

Electronic components interface with the 40-pin zero insertion force (ZIF) socket or low leadtime socket adapters manufactured by Chiplytics for a wide range of package types.

#### Cross-Platform Application

The Chiplytics desktop app automates data collection and stores signatures locally or over a secure cloud database for scalable data management and analysis.

### Fast, Repeatable Signature Acquisition

Each component has a unique signature based on its electrical characteristics, material composition, and subtle manufacturing process variations.

### Homogeneity Analysis

Signatures can be screened for same-ness using machine learning, revealing differences between components such as in manufacturer, performance, even down to date codes.

### Use Cases

- Batch Homogeneity Evaluation: Collect power signatures to evaluate homogeneity among a batch of components
- Authenticity Verification: Compare signatures against a golden sample or run more extensive testing (e.g. destructive) on a subset to verify authenticity of the entire batch
- Component Health Prediction: Capture power signature profiles of aged reference components to predict the health and lifetime remaining of other samples
- Pre/Post Analysis: Quantify differences between parts before and after failure analysis, radiation hardening, or reverse engineering testing
- Supply Chain Traceability: Trace components throughout the supply chain using unique power signature fingerprints for provenance tracking

## Doctrine
# Targeting doctrine brief (inject into every dossier & company agent — do NOT re-read the corpus)

Distilled from Jordan Crawford's Cannonball/Blueprint corpus, 2026-07-23.

1. **Pain-Qualified Segment beats ICP.** Never target firmographics ("50-200 person SaaS").
   Target companies in an observable painful SITUATION right now. The situation must be
   provable from public data.
2. **Existential Data Point (EDP).** For each niche, name the single number that kills the
   company if the problem stays unfixed (e.g. equipment utilization 60% vs 80% margins;
   fleet utilization <70% = crisis). Every dossier must state the EDP of its CTO's target
   situation: "what does it cost a company, in what metric, to leave this unfixed?"
3. **Jobs are confessions.** A job posting is a company publicly describing its own pain.
   We match jobs whose EXISTENCE signals a problem the CTO has already fixed — not jobs the
   CTO would apply to. "First Head of Engineering" = no technical leadership. "Integration
   Engineer (Epic, HL7)" = drowning in healthcare plumbing.
4. **The message is a redescription of the targeting.** If targeting is precise, the message
   writes itself by describing what we found. The reader should think "how do they know that?"
5. **Message-as-gift (GTM Shift letter standard).** Conversational, unhurried, 4–6 short
   sentences. Opens with a verified surprising fact. Every claim traces to a named public
   source. Valuable even if never answered. One light question, under 15 words. Bans: "I
   noticed", flattery, "hope you're well", meeting asks, personal details about the recipient
   as the opener. Lead with the CTO's expertise matched to the company's evident problem.
6. **Niche = Industry × Situation × Unique Angle** — all three specific (Ben Horowitz
   principle: not best botanist; best Japanese botanist specializing in Zen gardens).
7. **Insight tests**: counterintuitive (would other CTOs argue?), specific (exact tools/
   patterns), quantifiable (before/after numbers), demonstrable (showable in 5 minutes).
8. **Evidence honesty.** Actions > words > database records. Aggregator/API data (Blitz,
   FullEnrich) never corroborates itself — load-bearing claims need the person's or
   company's OWN public artifacts. Date every claim; label estimates as estimates.
9. **Dropping a bad fit is success.** Never pad a list to hit a count.
