## SBIR/STTR record (their own abstracts)

### LIDAR Scanning, Point Cloud Analytics, and 3D Data Management in Support of Rapid Airfield Assessments — Air Force 2022 ($750,000)
Candelytics is developing a proprietary LIDAR-based software solution that enables the seamless collection, analytics, and sharing of 3D data. Our mission is to democratize access to 3D data and revolutionize human interaction with the physical world. Specifically, Candelytics is building software that provides advanced 3D visualization capabilities; automatic linear, area, and volumetric measurements; A/B differential analysis; and 3D object segmentation and recognition. Our company is also developing a middleware digital infrastructure to store, tag, and share 3D data on a browser-based platform. Candelytics’ 3D data repository will leverage both real-world and synthetic data to develop advanced 3D applications powered by machine learning models such as 3D search, object recognition, spatial awareness, and predictive maintenance and analysis. As the winners of the DoD's inaugural Defense Innovation Accelerator - a National Security Innovation Network program - Candelytics has demonstrated our technology to the U.S. Coast Guard via nine virtual demonstrations and two on-site demonstrations at Coast Guard Sectors San Diego and Miami. Participants also included representatives from the DEA, CBP, and Tactical Law Enforcement (TACLET) teams. Candelytics has developed prototype software that allowed Coast Guard operators to execute basic change detection and volumetric calculations on point cloud data and added features that allowed users to annotate voids and suspicious areas that could represent smuggling compartments. We have also demonstrated our technology for the U.S. Air Force with a focus on expeditionary airfield construction, the Rapid Airfield Damage Assessment System (RADAS) program, and airfield repair. Air Force civil engineer units and airfield managers have encouraged Candelytics to further develop and test our technology to allow airfield construction and recovery teams to identify Foreign Object Debris (FOD) and conduct damage assessments (e.g., crater or hurricane analytics) more quickly and effectively. Having performed more than 250 customer discovery interviews, Candelytics has developed a deep understanding of the capabilities and technical gaps within the field of 3D data. We have discovered is a critical need to develop a tactical platform that will enable users to quickly view large 3D data files, share this information in near real-time, and generate machine-learning models that can provide predictive analytics capabilities. The Coast Guard and Air Force use cases highlight just two of many pain points that Candelytics' software solution can address. The coming revolution in 3D data will fundamentally alter the way humans interact with the physical world. Candelytics is developing the digital infrastructure that will make 3D data more accessible and insightful for dual-use commercial and government stakeholders.

## Doctrine
# Targeting doctrine brief (inject into every dossier & company agent — do NOT re-read the corpus)

Distilled from Jordan Crawford's Cannonball/Blueprint corpus, 2026-07-23.

1. **Pain-Qualified Segment beats ICP.** Never target firmographics ("50-200 person SaaS").
   Target companies in an observable painful SITUATION right now. The situation must be
   provable from public data.
2. **Existential Data Point (EDP).** For each niche, name the single number that kills the
   company if the problem stays unfixed (e.g. equipment utilization 60% vs 80% margins;
   fleet utilization <70% = crisis). Every dossier must state the EDP of its CTO's target
   situation: "what does it cost a company, in what metric, to leave this unfixed?"
3. **Jobs are confessions.** A job posting is a company publicly describing its own pain.
   We match jobs whose EXISTENCE signals a problem the CTO has already fixed — not jobs the
   CTO would apply to. "First Head of Engineering" = no technical leadership. "Integration
   Engineer (Epic, HL7)" = drowning in healthcare plumbing.
4. **The message is a redescription of the targeting.** If targeting is precise, the message
   writes itself by describing what we found. The reader should think "how do they know that?"
5. **Message-as-gift (GTM Shift letter standard).** Conversational, unhurried, 4–6 short
   sentences. Opens with a verified surprising fact. Every claim traces to a named public
   source. Valuable even if never answered. One light question, under 15 words. Bans: "I
   noticed", flattery, "hope you're well", meeting asks, personal details about the recipient
   as the opener. Lead with the CTO's expertise matched to the company's evident problem.
6. **Niche = Industry × Situation × Unique Angle** — all three specific (Ben Horowitz
   principle: not best botanist; best Japanese botanist specializing in Zen gardens).
7. **Insight tests**: counterintuitive (would other CTOs argue?), specific (exact tools/
   patterns), quantifiable (before/after numbers), demonstrable (showable in 5 minutes).
8. **Evidence honesty.** Actions > words > database records. Aggregator/API data (Blitz,
   FullEnrich) never corroborates itself — load-bearing claims need the person's or
   company's OWN public artifacts. Date every claim; label estimates as estimates.
9. **Dropping a bad fit is success.** Never pad a list to hit a count.
