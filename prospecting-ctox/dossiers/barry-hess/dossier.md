## The EDP

**Months-to-field versus funded months remaining.** A $5–10M defense tech company lives on fixed periods of performance; when time-to-field exceeds the funded contract window, the program dies in the DoD "valley of death" — no Phase III/production transition means that revenue line goes to zero, and for a company this size one dead transition is often the company. His own quantification: a previous engagement took "a program from prototype to fielded capability in 6 months, cutting 3 months off the planned timeline by eliminating redundant build cycles" [self-reported] — i.e., he sells back the months that decide whether the transition window is made or missed.
