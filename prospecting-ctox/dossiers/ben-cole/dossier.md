## Niche statement

Fractional technical-operations leader for 20–200-person regulated B2B SaaS companies and game studios whose growth has outrun the ops layer (Industry) — engaged at the moment builds depend on one person, cloud spend drifts unowned, and an enterprise buyer, auditor, or PE board demands SOC 2/CJIS evidence and cost visibility the team can't produce (Situation) — owning the shared platform/DevSecOps/FinOps layer *underneath* the CTO so product engineers stop firefighting, a playbook he ran at Expedia (0→95), Pokémon (2→32, 320M users), Prytania (0→35, 4 studios), and Lexipol (first-attempt SOC 2 Type II, −22% AWS) (Unique Angle).

## The EDP

**Unowned technical operations silently converts runway into waste.** The numbers, from his own published record: a cloud bill can double in six months with nobody flagging it because nobody owns it (his post, 2026-07-07); ~20% of cloud spend is typically recoverable once someone owns it (he cut AWS 22% at Lexipol); a baseline tooling stack runs ~$250/employee/month that founders leave out of runway models (his post, 2026-06-29); and enterprise revenue is frozen until SOC 2 Type II exists — a company that leaves this layer unowned at 20–200-person scale loses months of runway and its largest deals before the problem ever appears as a line item the board can see.
