## Niche statement

Governance-focused fractional CTO for growth-stage independent and charter schools (~800–2,000 students) whose boards cannot answer basic technology-risk questions — schools that have outgrown reactive outsourced IT but have no technology leadership — delivering a 90-day board-level cybersecurity/AI/vendor-governance clarity plan and an enrollment-aligned 12–24 month roadmap *without replacing the school's existing IT vendors* (plus a proven sideline rescuing organizations off dying legacy phone systems onto Microsoft Teams Phone).

- **Industry:** independent & charter K-12 schools (secondary: SMBs/media companies on legacy telephony).
- **Situation:** enrollment growing, IT fully outsourced to vendors nobody oversees, AI tools spreading faster than policy, board asking questions leadership can't answer — often triggered by a peer school's breach or an auto-renewing vendor contract.
- **Unique angle:** he sells *governance oversight of the vendors*, not IT services — an independent board-level layer that keeps every existing vendor in place; his 90-day deliverable is a document the board can read, not a system the school must run.

## The EDP (existential data point)

**Tuition-funded schools die by enrollment, and incident-recovery time is what moves it.** His own 2026-05-26 case: two schools, same vendor breach — the governed school recovered in **days**, the ungoverned one scrambled for **weeks**, including weeks of unmanaged parent communication. For an independent school of 800–2,000 students at roughly $10–30k tuition, losing even ~5% of families after a mishandled incident is roughly **$0.6M–$3M of annual recurring tuition revenue — more than most independent schools' entire operating margin** (estimate; tuition and margin figures are market-typical, not from his materials). The number that kills the company: **weeks-not-days of incident recovery in front of tuition-paying families.** Secondary EDP for the Teksavvy motion: an end-of-life PBX with "minimal support, scarce parts" is unbudgeted downtime waiting to happen, while migration cuts telephony spend ~50% (teksavvy.co case).
