## CTO niche
Fractional CTO for US Series A/B defense tech startups of roughly 8-30 people building AI/ML, geospatial or sensor products for DoW and Intelligence Community customers - the team built the technology but needs cleared senior leadership (active TS/SCI) to get it procurement-ready and into warfighters' hands.
EDP: Months-to-field versus funded months remaining: when time-to-field outruns the period of performance, the program dies in the valley of death - and at 8-30 people one dead transition is often the company.
